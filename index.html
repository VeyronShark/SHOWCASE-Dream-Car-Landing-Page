Inspiration

A LEGACY REIMAGINED.

Some cars are designed to be driven. Others are designed to change what driving means.

The McLaren W1 carries forward the philosophy of the legendary McLaren F1 and McLaren P1. Three generations, one relentless pursuit: to push the boundaries of what a road-legal supercar can achieve.

Revealed on the 50th anniversary of McLaren's first Formula 1 World Championship victory, the W1 brings the brand's racing heritage into a new era of hybrid performance.

01 / POWERTRAIN

Powertrain — The heart of the W1

A new generation of hybrid power.

At the heart of the W1 is the MHP-8, an all-new 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 engine, working in harmony with an electric motor. Together, they produce 1,275 PS, delivering a combination of immense power and rapid throttle response.

The electric motor enhances acceleration and responsiveness, while the V8 delivers the unmistakable character of a high-performance combustion engine.

02 / AERODYNAMICS

Aerodynamics — Shaped by the air

Downforce is designed, not added.

The W1 uses a ground-effect aerodynamic concept, drawing on principles developed in Formula 1. Its sculpted bodywork, active front aero and McLaren Active Long Tail work together to manage airflow and generate downforce.

In Race mode, the Active Long Tail extends by 300 mm, increasing the aerodynamic working area and contributing to up to 1,000 kg of downforce.
